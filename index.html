🎮 How to play
🖥️ PC: click game to lock mouse • WASD move • Space jump • Left-click break • Right-click place • 1–9 / wheel = blocks • F = fly
📱 Phone/tablet: left stick = move • drag right side = look • ⛏ break • 🧱 place • ▲ jump (hold to fly up) • FLY toggle
🌍 Multiplayer: share the room link — same room = same world. Breaks/places & chat sync live.
